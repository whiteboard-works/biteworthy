# Strategy and launch planning, 2026 (archive)

Three planning documents from June and July 2026, archived 2026-10-12 and
read-only. They were written when the product was "code-complete,
credential-gated" and before it launched in Durango; by October their
checklists disagreed with each other and with the log, so the open items
were folded into [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../roadmap.md) (_Launch_ and
_Growth bets_) and [`docs/launch-readiness.md`](../../launch-readiness.md).

| File                  | What it was                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `strategy-2026-h2.md` | The strategic review: the frequency diagnosis, the competitive wedge, the contribution-loop bet, the discovery-led expansion (§7), the three numbers on the wall. |
| `launch-plan.md`      | The two-track launch sequence: Track A manual provisioning in waves, Track B lower-the-gate SEO work and the restaurant QR program.                               |
| `six-month-plan.md`   | The strategy reversed into `[MANUAL]` and `[CODE]` tasks with the three steelmanned decisions and the decision gates.                                             |

What still holds from them is in [`docs/vision.md`](../../vision.md) and
the _Growth bets_ section of the roadmap. Read these for the reasoning
behind a bet, never for what is left to do.
